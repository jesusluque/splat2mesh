    Idol invents the sides the picture does not show; every seed invents them differently. With more than one seed, each is rendered from four sides with athenea and scored by DeepSeek against the picture, and the best is kept (about 4–5 min per seed on an 8 GB GPU).

    Outputs

    Parts by hand (optional)

    Instead of the automatic list: material: part, part; material: part with the library's names, e.g. leather: the boots, the belt; polished steel: the helmet.
