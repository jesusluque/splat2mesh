    What do you start from?

    Input

    Idol works at 1024 px, so an upscale gives it nothing; the restorer adds sharper detail inside the same pixels. About 4 minutes; needs Iris-3B installed locally.

    3D (Idol)

    Idol invents the sides the picture does not show; every seed invents them differently. With more than one seed, each is rendered from four sides with athenea and scored by DeepSeek against the picture, and the best is kept (about 4–5 min per seed on an 8 GB GPU).

    Advanced (Idol)

    Defaults are Idol's own. Seeds tried: 42, 1, 2, 3… in that order.
